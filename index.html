Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

Ethio Radio Live ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data within the Ethio Radio Live Android application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). No account registration is required to use this app.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

This app uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data, such as your Advertising ID and approximate location, to provide personalized ads. You can review Google's policy here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Microphone Usage (Recording Feature)

Ethio Radio Live includes a recording feature. This requires access to your device's microphone/audio input. Crucially: All recordings are stored locally on your device's storage. We do not upload, transmit, or share your audio recordings with any external servers.

4. Show Reminders & Local Notifications

The app allows you to set reminders for radio programs. This data (program name and time) is stored only on your device and is used solely to trigger local notifications. We do not track your listening habits or reminder settings.

5. Data Storage

Your "Favourites" and "Recordings" are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data may be deleted unless you have manually backed up your recordings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
